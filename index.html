The Story

Planted in Promise is more than a skin studio — it's a place where clients come to be cared for, not just treated. Every service is performed with intention, skill, and a genuine belief that you deserve to feel beautiful in your own skin.

Licensed in December 2024 and rooted in faith, this studio was built on the conviction that when God plants a promise in you, you bloom on His timing. That's the heart behind every appointment.

Serving the Temperance, Monroe, Lambertville, and Toledo communities — a welcoming, peaceful space for everyone who walks through the door.
